Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about project timelines, revisions, combined services, and ongoing creative support.

How long does a project usually take?

Project timing depends on the approved scope, complexity, quantity, materials, production requirements, feedback and approval speed, and requested deadline. The expected schedule will be listed in your estimate or project agreement. Rush scheduling may be available when design and production capacity allow.

How many revisions are included?

The included revision allowance will be listed in your estimate or agreement. Revisions must remain within the approved project scope. Additional concepts, major direction changes, or revisions beyond the included allowance may require a change request, adjusted deadline, revised estimate, or additional invoice.

Can I combine multiple services in one project?

Yes. You can request websites, logos, business cards, signs, banners, print products, social media graphics, email banners, and custom design in the same project form. Related services may be organized under one coordinated estimate, agreement, and schedule.

Do you offer ongoing or recurring design services?

Yes. Website maintenance, social media graphics, email banners, recurring marketing materials, and other creative support may be scheduled as one-time or ongoing work. Deliverables, frequency, revision limits, response times, and billing terms will be listed in the applicable estimate or service plan.
