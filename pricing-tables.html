How Pricing Works

The Creative Studio uses scope-based pricing. The tables below show the pricing structure for each service without inventing fixed public prices that may not match the actual work, quantity, material, deadline, or production requirements.

Website & Digital Pricing

Digital projects are quoted according to page count, functionality, integrations, content readiness, support needs, and delivery schedule.

Digital Services
Service Pricing Type Primary Pricing Factors Common Deliverables Billing Method
Website Design Custom Quote Page count, complexity, forms, ecommerce, integrations, content, accessibility, deadline Responsive pages, navigation, forms, service pages, contact tools Deposit, milestone, and final invoice
Landing Page Custom Quote Sections, conversion features, copy readiness, forms, analytics, integrations Single-page campaign or lead-generation page One-time project invoice
Website Maintenance One-Time or Recurring Update frequency, number of pages, troubleshooting, content changes, response time Content updates, fixes, additions, maintenance, support Per request or recurring service plan
Forms & Interactive Features Scope-Based Form length, validation, uploads, database/API connection, automation Contact, order, upload, booking, intake, and customer forms Added to website or quoted separately
Website Content Support Scope-Based Number of pages, research, rewriting, SEO needs, approval rounds Page copy, headings, calls to action, service descriptions Per page or combined project quote

Branding & Graphic Design Pricing

Branding and design pricing depends on the number of concepts, revisions, file types, versions, and intended uses.

Creative Services
Service Pricing Type Primary Pricing Factors Common Deliverables Billing Method
Logo Design Custom Quote Concept count, revisions, complexity, variations, hidden meanings, brand research Primary logo, color variations, transparent files, print-ready files Project deposit and final invoice
Brand Identity Materials Package Quote Number of items, visual system, brand guide depth, file variations Logo system, colors, fonts, business materials, brand guide Coordinated package estimate
Flyers Custom Quote Size, sides, content volume, images, revisions, print requirements Digital or print-ready flyer designs Design fee plus production when requested
Custom Graphic Design Scope-Based Dimensions, concepts, revisions, file formats, complexity, urgency Custom graphics, layouts, promotional pieces, specialty designs Per project or bundled quote

Print, Signs & Product Pricing

Physical products include a design component and, when requested, a separate production quote based on size, material, finish, quantity, and fulfillment.

Production Services
Product Pricing Type Primary Pricing Factors Common Options Quote Structure

Yard Signs Size + Quantity Dimensions, material, sides, stakes, quantity, deadline Single or double-sided, standard or custom size Design plus per-project production quote
Yard Banners Square Foot + Finish Width, height, material, hemming, grommets, pole pockets, installation Indoor, outdoor, event, storefront, custom size Design plus square-foot and finishing quote
Magnets Size + Quantity Size, shape, material thickness, color, quantity, intended use Promotional, vehicle, service, custom magnet Design plus size-and-quantity production quote
Greeting Cards Design + Quantity Format, folds, dimensions, paper, finish, quantity, envelopes Flat, folded, invitations, announcements, thank-you cards Design fee plus print quote
Postcards Design + Quantity Size, sides, paper, finish, quantity, mailing support 4×6, 5×7, 6×9, custom, printing and mailing Design fee plus print or mailing quote

Printing, materials, vendor charges, shipping, delivery, installation, and applicable taxes are listed separately when they apply.

Additional Fees & Pricing Adjustments

These items are not automatically charged. They are included only when the project requires them and are shown in writing before approval.

Project Adjustments
Adjustment When It Applies Pricing Method Customer Approval
Rush Scheduling Work requested faster than the normal production schedule Availability-Based Shown in revised estimate before work begins
Additional Revisions Revisions beyond the allowance listed in the estimate or agreement Per Round or Scope Change Approved before extra revision work continues
New Concepts or Direction Changes Major creative changes outside the approved direction Revised Scope Quote Requires written change approval
Vendor or Material Changes Different paper, finish, material, dimensions, or production method Updated Production Quote Approved before production or purchasing
Shipping, Delivery, or Installation Physical fulfillment beyond digital delivery Destination-Based Listed before fulfillment
Cancellation or Rework Costs Work stopped or changed after design, purchasing, proof approval, or production Actual Work and Vendor Cost Handled under the approved agreement and policies
