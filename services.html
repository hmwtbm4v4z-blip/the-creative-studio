Service Questions

Common answers about selecting services, combining projects, revisions, and ongoing work.

Can I order more than one service at the same time?

Yes. The project form allows you to select multiple services and explain how they should work together. Related services may be organized into one estimate, project schedule, agreement, or payment plan.

Do you offer printing and production?

Physical products may include printing or production coordination. Final pricing depends on size, material, quantity, finish, vendor availability, shipping, and delivery.

Are revisions included?

The included revision allowance is identified in the estimate or agreement. Work outside the approved scope may require a change request, revised estimate, or new deadline.

Can I request a service that is not listed?

Yes. Choose Custom Graphic Design and describe the project in detail. The request will be reviewed to determine whether it can be completed and how it should be quoted.
