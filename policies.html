Privacy Policy

The Creative Studio may collect information that customers voluntarily provide through project forms, contact forms, booking pages, customer portals, file uploads, surveys, emails, phone calls, text messages, invoices, agreements, and payment-related interactions.

Information We May Collect

  • Names, business names, email addresses, phone numbers, billing addresses, and preferred contact methods.
  • Project descriptions, branding preferences, requested services, quantities, deadlines, files, comments, approvals, and customer-provided content.
  • Order, estimate, project, proof, agreement, invoice, payment-status, delivery, and support information.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, page activity, access times, and basic security logs when analytics or secure portal services are enabled.

How Information May Be Used

  • To respond to questions and provide requested services.
  • To prepare estimates, agreements, invoices, proofs, production files, deliveries, and customer support.
  • To communicate about project status, approvals, payments, deadlines, deliveries, and account activity.
  • To protect the website, prevent fraud, improve services, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations.

Service Providers

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to complete a project or operate the business, such as payment processors, hosting providers, email providers, printers, manufacturers, shipping carriers, software providers, or professional advisers. Each provider may maintain its own policies.

Payment Information

The Creative Studio should not store complete payment-card numbers in website forms or browser storage. Electronic payments should be processed through an authorized payment provider. The payment provider may collect and process payment information under its own privacy and security terms.

Data Retention and Security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for active projects, customer service, accounting, legal obligations, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and business records. Reasonable safeguards should be used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Cookies and Analytics

The website may use necessary browser storage, cookies, analytics, or similar technologies to remember preferences, support secure sessions, understand website use, and improve performance. A cookie notice and consent controls should be added before nonessential analytics or advertising technologies are enabled.

Children

The website and services are intended for businesses and adults. The Creative Studio does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

Privacy Requests

Customers may contact The Creative Studio to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information, subject to records that must be retained for contracts, payments, accounting, security, disputes, or legal requirements.

Communications Policy

By submitting contact information, a customer authorizes The Creative Studio to respond about the inquiry, order, estimate, project, proof, agreement, invoice, delivery, support request, or account activity.

  • Email is the default written communication method unless another method is agreed upon.
  • Phone calls or text messages are used only when the customer provides a phone number and appropriate permission.
  • Customers are responsible for keeping contact information current.
  • Marketing emails should include a clear way to unsubscribe. Transactional messages about active work may still be sent when needed.
  • Standard carrier message or data rates may apply to text messages.

General Service Terms

Services are provided according to the written scope in the accepted estimate, proposal, invoice, agreement, project record, or change order. Verbal discussions do not modify the approved scope unless confirmed in writing.

  • The customer must provide accurate, complete, and timely information needed to perform the work.
  • The Creative Studio may decline work that is illegal, deceptive, infringing, abusive, discriminatory, unsafe, technically unreasonable, or outside available capabilities.
  • Work may be paused when required information, approval, content, files, access, or payment is missing.
  • Third-party services and vendors may be used when reasonably necessary to complete the project.

Estimates, Quotes & Proposals

  • Estimates are based on the information available when prepared.
  • An estimate is not a final invoice unless clearly identified as one.
  • Estimates may expire on the date shown because vendor, material, production, software, shipping, or service costs can change.
  • Taxes, licenses, hosting, domains, stock assets, printing, shipping, installation, rush service, or outside-vendor costs may be listed separately.
  • Acceptance confirms approval of the stated scope, pricing, assumptions, exclusions, payment schedule, and timeline.

Payments, Deposits & Billing

  • A deposit or advance payment may be required before work, scheduling, purchasing, printing, fabrication, or production begins.
  • Payment schedules may include deposits, milestones, progress invoices, recurring invoices, or a final balance.
  • Payment is due by the date shown on the invoice or agreement.
  • Work, delivery, final files, publishing, transfer, launch, or additional service may be paused while a balance is overdue.
  • Customers should not send complete card information by email, text message, contact form, or unprotected file.
  • Chargebacks or payment disputes should be preceded by a good-faith attempt to resolve the issue directly with The Creative Studio.

Any late fee, collection cost, returned-payment charge, or finance term must be specifically stated in the applicable written agreement and permitted by law.

Scheduling, Turnaround & Deadlines

Project timelines are estimates unless a written agreement expressly guarantees a specific completion date.

  • Scheduling generally begins after required payment, information, files, and approvals are received.
  • Customer delays may move the delivery date and affect production or vendor availability.
  • Rush requests are subject to availability and may involve additional charges.
  • Delays caused by vendors, carriers, outages, severe weather, emergencies, illness, supply shortages, or events outside reasonable control may require a revised schedule.

Revisions, Scope Changes & Additional Work

  • The included revision allowance is stated in the estimate, proposal, package, or agreement.
  • Revisions must be submitted clearly and, when practical, grouped into one written response.
  • A revision does not include a new concept, new service, changed project direction, new dimensions, additional pages, added products, or work outside the approved scope.
  • Additional work may require a change request, revised estimate, new deposit, additional invoice, or adjusted deadline.
  • Changes requested after approval or production may require rework, reprinting, repurchasing, or additional vendor costs.

Proofing & Customer Approval

The customer is responsible for carefully reviewing every proof before approval, including spelling, grammar, contact information, names, dates, pricing, colors, dimensions, layout, quantities, links, images, legal statements, and other project details.

  • Approval authorizes The Creative Studio to proceed with the approved version.
  • Approval may be recorded through email, a proof page, customer portal, signed agreement, or another written method.
  • Customer-approved errors generally are not considered production defects.
  • Corrections requested after approval may involve additional design, production, vendor, shipping, or replacement costs.
  • Screen colors may differ from printed or manufactured colors because devices, inks, papers, materials, lighting, and production methods vary.

Customer-Provided Content & Materials

The customer represents that they have the right to use all text, trademarks, logos, photographs, illustrations, videos, fonts, data, music, designs, and other materials they provide.

  • The customer is responsible for the accuracy and legality of supplied content.
  • The customer is responsible for obtaining required releases, licenses, permissions, disclosures, and regulatory approvals.
  • Low-quality or incomplete files may affect quality, timing, price, or the ability to complete the work.
  • The Creative Studio may reject material reasonably believed to violate rights, laws, platform rules, or public safety.

Intellectual Property & Usage Rights

Ownership and usage rights are governed by the applicable estimate, agreement, invoice, or license. Unless otherwise stated in writing:

  • The Creative Studio retains ownership of preliminary concepts, rejected concepts, working files, templates, methods, code libraries, processes, and reusable design elements.
  • Final customer usage rights become effective after all required payments are completed.
  • Third-party fonts, stock images, software, plugins, themes, code, music, or other licensed assets remain subject to their original license terms.
  • Editable source files are included only when specifically listed in the approved scope.
  • The customer may not resell, sublicense, redistribute, trademark, or claim exclusive ownership of third-party assets beyond the rights granted by their licenses.

Portfolio, Credit & Public Display

Unless confidentiality is agreed upon in writing, The Creative Studio may request permission to display completed public-facing work in a portfolio, website, social media post, proposal, case study, or marketing material. Sensitive customer information, private files, unpublished campaigns, and confidential business information should not be disclosed.

A customer may request confidential treatment before work begins or before the project is published. Any required nondisclosure agreement should be completed in writing.

Cancellation, Refunds & Project Inactivity

  • Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing.
  • Payments may be applied to completed work, reserved time, administrative work, purchased materials, software, stock assets, vendor charges, production, shipping, and other nonrecoverable costs.
  • Deposits are refundable only when the applicable estimate, agreement, and law permit a refund.
  • Approved, printed, fabricated, shipped, downloaded, transferred, licensed, published, launched, or custom-made work may not be returnable.
  • If a customer stops responding, the project may be placed on hold. Restarting may depend on schedule availability, updated pricing, and payment of outstanding amounts.
  • Any eligible refund should be returned through an appropriate method after completed work and nonrecoverable costs are reconciled.

Printing, Manufacturing & Production

  • Production begins only after required approval and payment.
  • Small variations in color, placement, trimming, dimensions, finish, material, texture, or quantity may occur within normal production tolerances.
  • Vendor estimates and availability may change before an order is placed.
  • The customer must report a suspected production defect promptly and preserve the affected products, packaging, photographs, shipping labels, and quantities for review.
  • Reprints or replacements are evaluated based on the approved proof, production specifications, vendor findings, and documented defect.

Shipping, Delivery & Customer Pickup

  • Shipping and delivery dates are estimates unless expressly guaranteed.
  • Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate address.
  • Carrier delays, weather, address errors, failed delivery attempts, theft after delivery, or damage outside reasonable control may require a carrier claim.
  • Customers should inspect packages promptly and report visible damage with photographs of the product, packaging, and shipping label.
  • Pickup orders must be collected within the communicated period. Long-term storage, rescheduling, or disposal terms should be confirmed before production.

Website Design, Hosting & Maintenance

  • The customer is responsible for timely content, account access, approvals, domain decisions, and legal or regulatory requirements.
  • Domains, hosting, email accounts, plugins, themes, APIs, software subscriptions, stock assets, and third-party services may involve separate recurring fees.
  • Search-engine ranking, traffic, sales, accessibility certification, uninterrupted operation, or compatibility with every future browser, device, platform, or third-party change cannot be guaranteed.
  • Security updates, backups, monitoring, content changes, and technical support are included only when stated in the maintenance agreement.
  • The customer is responsible for maintaining lawful privacy notices, cookie disclosures, accessibility practices, industry disclosures, and content after handoff unless those services are included.
  • Website launches and transfers may require payment of the final balance and confirmation of the receiving account information.

Digital Files, Delivery & Storage

  • Final files are delivered in the formats listed in the approved scope.
  • Editable, layered, raw, source, working, or native files are included only when specifically stated.
  • Customers should download, verify, and securely back up final files.
  • Project files may be archived for a limited period, but permanent storage is not guaranteed unless a retention service is agreed upon.
  • Recreating or retrieving archived work may involve additional charges.

Corrections, Defects & Limited Warranty

The Creative Studio will review promptly reported concerns and determine an appropriate resolution based on the approved scope, proof, delivered files, production specifications, vendor findings, and supporting evidence.

  • A verified studio-caused error may be corrected within a reasonable time.
  • Customer-approved errors, customer-supplied errors, normal production variation, third-party outages, platform changes, or misuse are not treated as studio defects.
  • The available remedy may be correction, replacement, reprint, service credit, partial refund, refund, or another reasonable resolution, depending on the circumstances and applicable agreement.

Third-Party Services & Limitations

The Creative Studio may rely on third-party providers for payments, hosting, domains, email, software, printing, manufacturing, shipping, analytics, storage, and other services. Those providers control their own systems, availability, policies, licenses, pricing, and performance.

Any limitation of liability, indemnification obligation, dispute process, governing law, or attorney-fee provision should be stated in a written customer agreement reviewed for compliance with applicable law.

Changes to These Policies

These policies may be updated as the website, services, vendors, legal requirements, or business practices change. The effective date at the top of the page identifies the current posted version. Material changes should not retroactively replace signed project terms unless the customer agrees in writing.

Before your project begins

Confirm the essentials together before design work starts.

Your project brief

Provide your business name, primary contact, audience, requested services, preferred style, existing brand files, and examples. Identify any colors, imagery, or wording to avoid.

Scope and decision maker

Confirm deliverables, sizes, quantities, page counts, revision allowance, payment milestones, and target dates in the written estimate. Choose one contact to combine feedback and approve decisions.

Content readiness

Supply final copy and usable images before the relevant design stage. Tell us about missing materials early so the schedule can be adjusted.

How to send useful feedback

One clear response helps keep your project moving.

Identify the version

Include the project name and proof version. Refer to a page, section, or item so the change can be located easily.

Combine your changes

Collect feedback from everyone who needs to review the project. Send a numbered list of changes, with replacement wording written exactly as it should appear.

Revision or new request?

Adjusting wording or placement within the agreed direction may be a revision. A new logo direction, extra web page, different print size, or additional service can change the scope. Confirm any additional price and timing in writing before that work begins.

Your final approval checklist

Review the complete proof at the intended size before authorizing the next stage.

For every project

Check spelling, names, phone numbers, email addresses, website links, prices, dates, quantities, and the correct logo. Confirm that the proof version is the one you want produced.

For print and signage

Check front and back, orientation, dimensions, material, trim and safe areas. Test QR codes from the proof. Ask about a physical sample or color proof if an exact color match is essential; availability and cost need to be confirmed.

For websites

Review desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, contact details, forms, downloads, booking links, and any checkout flow included in the scope. Confirm the destination domain and who is authorized to approve launch.

Record your decision

Send written approval identifying the version, or send your change list. Approval to print and approval to publish are separate decisions when a project includes both.

Website launch and handoff

A finished design and a live website have different requirements.

Before launch

Confirm the approved version, domain, hosting account, account access, and launch timing. Identify any existing website or email service that must continue operating during the change.

Your handoff record

Confirm which files, account invitations, setup notes, licenses, and training are included. Store your own copy of final files and account recovery information securely.

Account access

Use account invitations with appropriate permissions when supported. Do not send passwords, card details, or private recovery codes through a general contact form. Arrange a suitable transfer method for any access that cannot be delegated.

After handoff

Check the delivered files and links and report anything missing. New features, ongoing content edits, and maintenance follow the separately agreed scope.

Ongoing maintenance and renewals

Know who is responsible for keeping each service running.

Confirm coverage

A maintenance agreement should identify covered tasks, contact methods, support availability, and any included time. Website design alone does not include continuous monitoring or unlimited updates.

Renewals and ownership

Confirm who pays for and renews the domain, hosting, business email, software, plugins, and other subscriptions. Keep billing contacts and recovery details current.

Backups and changes

Confirm who makes backups, what is included, and how restoration is requested. Tell us about edits made by another provider so troubleshooting can account for those changes.

Ending a service

Use the cancellation method and notice period in your service agreement. Discuss file export and account transfer before the service ends; do not assume a cancelled hosting account will retain a working copy.

Signs, decals and printed products

Confirm the intended use before selecting materials.

Choosing materials

Tell us whether the item will be used indoors or outdoors, its surface, exposure, expected lifespan, and installation needs. Confirm product specifications with the estimate.

Installation and care

Follow the supplied manufacturer instructions for surface preparation, application, cleaning, removal, and storage. Design or supply of a product does not include installation unless it is listed in the scope.

Vehicle products

Confirm surface compatibility and care instructions before applying decals or magnets. A magnet requires a suitable magnetic surface; suitability and adhesion should be checked before use.

Report a concern

Provide the order reference, product type, affected quantity, photographs, and a description of the issue. Keep the product and packaging while the concern is reviewed.

Questions, corrections and next steps

Help us identify the issue and the outcome you are looking for.

Send the details

Include your project or invoice reference, the approved proof or page URL, what happened, and supporting screenshots or photographs. For website issues, include your device, browser, and steps to reproduce the problem.

Request a resolution

Explain whether you need a correction, missing file, production review, billing clarification, or cancellation review. The existing agreement and the policies above guide the response.

Use the right page

Use Customer Support for technical help, Change Request for additional work, or Resolution for a project concern. Avoid including sensitive account or payment information in the message.

ChatGPT & AI-Assisted Work

The Creative Studio may use ChatGPT and other AI tools to assist with brainstorming, draft copy, design exploration, code, and troubleshooting. This studio policy explains our proposed approach to client work; it is not an OpenAI policy or an endorsement by OpenAI. It remains part of this page’s policy draft.

Human review and honest presentation

AI-assisted work is reviewed and refined before delivery. OpenAI requires evaluation of output for accuracy and suitability and prohibits presenting AI-generated output as human-generated. AI can produce errors; it is not a substitute for professional advice. See OpenAI’s Terms of Use.

Our review includes relevant factual claims, client details, visual quality, and functionality within the agreed scope. Client proof approval remains required. AI assistance does not remove our responsibility to deliver the agreed work.

Client materials and permission

OpenAI requires the rights and permissions needed to submit material to its services. Before providing client content to an AI tool, we will confirm that its use is authorized and consistent with the project’s confidentiality requirements. Please identify restricted materials before work begins. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive personal records for use in AI prompts.

Privacy and data settings

Data handling depends on the product, account settings, and features used. Personal ChatGPT accounts offer a setting to exclude new conversations from model training; this does not mean conversations are never stored. See OpenAI’s Data Controls FAQ.

OpenAI states that ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, and API business data is not used for training by default, unless the customer opts in. This is not a claim that every studio project uses one of those services. See OpenAI’s business data commitments.

Before uploading confidential content, we will agree on an appropriate workflow and check the applicable settings. Unnecessary identifying details should be removed. External apps and connected services may have separate data terms.

Commercial use, ownership and originality

OpenAI assigns its rights in output to the user to the extent allowed by law, but output may resemble other users’ results, and third-party material has separate terms. Client rights in our final deliverables follow the project agreement. AI assistance does not guarantee exclusivity, trademark availability, or clearance of third-party rights. See OpenAI’s content terms and our intellectual property policy.

Acceptable use

AI-assisted requests must comply with OpenAI’s current Usage Policies. We will decline prohibited uses, including fraud, impersonation, privacy violations, infringing activity, harmful content involving minors, and attempts to bypass safeguards. These examples are not the complete policy.

Your preferences and project scope

If you require limits on AI use, tell us before work begins so we can confirm a suitable approach, price, and timeline in writing. AI use does not change the agreed revision allowance, payment terms, or approval process. Ask us about AI involvement in a specific deliverable through Contact the studio.
