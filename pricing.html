Pricing Questions

Clear answers about custom estimates, combined services, revisions, deposits, and recurring creative support.

Why are exact public prices not listed?

Each project is priced according to its actual scope. Design complexity, dimensions, quantity, materials, revision allowance, production requirements, vendor costs, shipping or delivery, and the requested deadline can all affect the final total. Your written estimate will identify the work and costs included.

Can I combine multiple services in one quote?

Yes. You can request websites, logos, business cards, signs, banners, print products, social media graphics, email banners, and custom design in the same project form. Related services may be organized under one coordinated estimate, agreement, and schedule.

How many revisions are included?

The included revision allowance will be listed in your estimate or agreement. Revisions must stay within the approved project scope. Additional concepts, major direction changes, or revisions beyond the included allowance may require a change request, adjusted deadline, revised estimate, or additional invoice.

Is a deposit required?

A deposit may be required before design time is reserved or before printing, materials, vendor purchasing, or production begins. The required amount, payment schedule, and remaining balance will be shown in your estimate, agreement, or invoice before work starts.

Do you offer ongoing or recurring services?

Yes. Website maintenance, social media graphics, email banners, recurring marketing materials, and other creative support may be scheduled as one-time or ongoing work. Deliverables, frequency, revision limits, response times, and billing terms will be listed in the applicable estimate or service plan.
